**Company:** Power Energy GmbH  
**Location:** Austria  
**Stage:** Pre-Seed

**Key Metrics:**

- Target Market: €1.65B (AT households + SMEs)

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### The Energy Challenge
1. **Dynamic Tariffs** - Austrian energy prices now fluctuate hourly (day-ahead market), but most consumers can't react manually
2. **Isolated Devices** - Battery storage, wallboxes, and heating systems operate independently
3. **Missed Savings** - €2,000-€5,000/year potential per household remains unused
4. **Regulatory Complexity** - EMS vs. Lieferant distinction confuses market entrants

### Market Gap
No Austrian platform offers end-to-end energy orchestration with:
- Real-time Smart-Meter integration
- Day-ahead tariff optimization
- Device-level control (battery, wallbox)

---

## 3. Solution

### Product Overview
**Power** = AI Energy Orchestrator for Austrian households & SMEs

**Core Modules:**
1. **Smart-Meter Gateway** - Ingests real-time consumption/production data
2. **Tarif-Engine** - Optimizes charging/discharging based on day-ahead prices
3. **Device Orchestrator** - Controls battery & wallbox for maximum savings

### Value Proposition
| Segment | Monthly Savings | Annual Savings |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Household (PV + Battery) | €30-€70 | €360-€840 |
| Household (PV only) | €15-€35 | €180-€420 |
| SME (with peak management) | €120-€450 | €1,440-€5,400 |
